707.ntest_r

Othello is a combinatorial game played on a 8x8 rectangular board with stones that are colored black on one side and white on the other side. Starting with a specified initial position of 4 stones, two black and two white in an X formation in the center of the board, players take turns making moves, starting with the black player. At each step, a stone with the color of the mover is placed on the board. The newly placed stone must capture, in between two of the mover's pieces, a contiguous line of opponent pieces, in any direction: horizontal, vertical, or diagonal. Capturing entails flipping the stones from the opponent's color to the mover's color. If a player can't move, they have to pass. If both players pass, then the match is over.

Official description is available at https://www.spec.org/cpu2026/Docs/benchmarks/707.ntest_r/707.ntest_r.html.

DISCLAIMER: All the SPEC results are "non-reportable". The measurements were taken in order to compare different compilers and compiler options, not different hardware, please do not attempt to make any comparisons of the different machines used. Moreover, we have broken various SPEC benchmarking rules, we have even patched some benchmark sources to be able to compile them with -fstrict-aliasing, build with -fno-common, and so forth. And the data may be just wrong, the scripts generating all the fancy graphs and tables have been known to contain bugs in the past. On the other hand, this is the data that we use to evaluate where GCC stands and we believe that overall this comparison of compilers is a useful one.
