766.femflow_r

The FemFlow program solves the compressible Navier-Stokes equations with high-order finite element methods. The equations are solved on locally refined meshes and use splitting methods, to treat the hyperbolic terms with a discontinuous Galerkin method on hexahedral elements of a 3D mesh (polynomial degree 4, over-integration on 6 points per direction) explicitly in time with Heun's method, while the parabolic terms are treated implicitly with a Crank-Nicolson scheme. The associated linear system is solved separately for velocity and temperature with a conjugate gradient method preconditioned by the matrix diagonal. The code relies on the deal.II finite element library, in particular its matrix-free infrastructure to quickly compute the underlying integrals with sum factorization.

Official description is available at https://www.spec.org/cpu2026/Docs/benchmarks/766.femflow_r/766.femflow_r.html.

DISCLAIMER: All the SPEC results are "non-reportable". The measurements were taken in order to compare different compilers and compiler options, not different hardware, please do not attempt to make any comparisons of the different machines used. Moreover, we have broken various SPEC benchmarking rules, we have even patched some benchmark sources to be able to compile them with -fstrict-aliasing, build with -fno-common, and so forth. And the data may be just wrong, the scripts generating all the fancy graphs and tables have been known to contain bugs in the past. On the other hand, this is the data that we use to evaluate where GCC stands and we believe that overall this comparison of compilers is a useful one.
